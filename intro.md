# Applied AI for Earth Sciences

**GEOL 522 / ATMS 522, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.** A fully online, asynchronous graduate course on machine learning and deep learning for Earth-science data: from a straight line fitted to river catchments in Week 1 to foundation models and operational AI in Week 11, with a semester project in between.

## How the course works

Each week has the same shape:

1. A short recorded lecture (20–30 minutes).
2. Two **core notebooks**, one idea each, with small predict-change-rerun exercises whose solutions sit at the end of the notebook.
3. A **concept-check quiz** on Canvas, taken before the lab; a few of its questions can only be answered by a student who did the core exercises.
4. One **lab**: a fully worked application of the week's idea to real Earth-science data, ending in two or three exercises; graded on completion.
5. **Homework** in most weeks: the lab's method on new data or with a twist, in a scaffolded notebook.

The week's landing page lists the order of work and the time each item takes. Week 1 begins with a setup page, because getting a working Python environment, on your own computer or in Google Colab, is part of the content of an applied AI course.

## Reading the notebooks

Every notebook opens with the same setup cell and the same download cell, uses the same vocabulary, and marks a few things with labeled callouts: *Check your understanding*, *Exercise*, *Warning*, *Earth-science aside*, *Data card*, and two that make the course's AI-assistant policy visible, *AI assistant: good use here* and *Write this yourself*.
